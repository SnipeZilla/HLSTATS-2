See how your HLstatsZ daemon copes with its load

  1. In hlstats.conf, set Profile 1 and restart HLstatsZ.
  2. Let it run while your game servers are busy. The daemon writes hlstatsz_profile.log in the profile folder, next to this page.
  3. Click Open log… and pick it in that folder, or drop it on this page. If there is also a hlstatsz_profile.old.log, open both together to see further back.
  4. When you are done, set Profile 0 and restart HLstatsZ.

The log is read in your browser and goes nowhere: this page works without internet. The two log files together never take more than about 10 MB.

Pauses and events

The daemon runs one event loop for every game server: during a pause, no server's lines are handled. They wait in the UDP buffer and are handled after. Click a row to zoom on it.
